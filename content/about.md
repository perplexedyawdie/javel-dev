I think about problems using a simple structure:

1. Context about the current situation.
2. A goal someone in that situation wants to achieve.

The impediment to that goal should be easy to infer. If not, the problem is not well understood or defined.

All my experiences over my career have helped me refine my intuition for what this looks like in practice.
